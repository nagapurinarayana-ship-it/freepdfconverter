IMAGE & FORM UPLOADS

Photo, signature and form image requirements

Prepare passport photos, signatures, thumb impressions and handwritten declarations for online forms by checking dimensions, file-size limits, format, background and framing.

Check the application first

There is no universal image requirement. Passport, visa, university, employer and government application forms can use different rules and those rules can change. Treat the destination's current instructions as authoritative.

Four things to check

  1. Dimensions: exact pixels, aspect ratio or physical size.
  2. Maximum file size: for example 20KB, 50KB or 100KB when specified.
  3. Format: use the format accepted by the destination.
  4. Background and framing: follow the stated background, head position and crop requirements.

Passport and ID photos

Use a clear front-facing photo with enough space around the head and shoulders. Manual framing is useful because a generic centre crop can cut off required areas.

FAQ

What is the correct photo size for a government exam?

It depends on the specific exam and application cycle. Use the current official instructions.

Can every image be compressed to exactly 20KB?

No. Different images have different complexity. A good compressor aims for the maximum while protecting quality.
