Frequently asked questions

Does this remove every identifier from a PDF?

No. It targets supported standard metadata fields. Other embedded data may require separate inspection.

Will the document content change?

The tool is designed to remove supported metadata while keeping the page content intact.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. Core processing happens locally in your browser.
