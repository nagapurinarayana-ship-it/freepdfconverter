Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate just one PDF page?

Yes. The tool supports page-range or selected-page rotation.

Does rotating a PDF reduce quality?

No. Rotation changes page orientation rather than converting the page into a screenshot.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. Core processing happens locally in your browser.
