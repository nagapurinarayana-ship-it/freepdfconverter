Frequently asked questions

Does cropping delete the original PDF?

No. The tool creates a new PDF and your source file remains on your device.

Can I crop all pages?

Yes. Apply consistent crop margins across the document or use the page controls provided by the tool.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. Core PDF processing happens locally in your browser.
