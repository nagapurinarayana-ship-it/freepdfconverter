Two ways to split

Extract range makes one PDF from consecutive pages. Split all makes one PDF per page and packages them in a ZIP.

Private by design

Your source PDF and generated pages remain on your device.

Extract only the PDF pages you need

After the PDF is loaded, its page count appears beside the file name. Enter the first and last page you want and choose Extract range. The browser creates a new PDF containing only those pages.

If you need one PDF for every original page, choose Split all to ZIP. The tool creates numbered single-page PDFs and bundles them so the browser needs to download only one ZIP archive.

When splitting is useful

  • Share one section of a report without sending the whole document.
  • Separate scanned forms or invoices into individual files.
  • Keep only a chapter or appendix from a larger reference PDF.
  • Prepare page-level files for a workflow that accepts one document at a time.

Read the split and extract PDF guide to choose the right method and avoid page-number mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract just one page?

Yes. Set the From and To page values to the same page number.

Does splitting upload the original PDF?

No. The source is parsed and copied inside your browser.

Why do all pages download as ZIP?

A ZIP keeps dozens of generated files together and avoids triggering a long series of separate browser downloads.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Use the Unlock PDF tool with the known password first, then split the unencrypted copy.
