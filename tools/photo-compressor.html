Useful presets

20KB and 50KB are useful for forms that impose small upload limits. 100KB, 200KB and 500KB are practical general-purpose targets.

Best quality

JPG and WebP normally reach small targets more easily than PNG. The tool reduces quality and, when necessary, dimensions rather than endlessly producing a very poor image.

Privacy

The image is read, resized and encoded in your browser. It is not uploaded to our server.

Free image compressor for application uploads

Many forms require a photograph below a particular file size. This tool lets you select a target such as 20KB, 50KB, 100KB, 200KB or 1MB and creates a smaller copy without changing the original file.

How it works

  1. Select your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Choose a target size and maximum width.
  3. Pick JPG, WebP or PNG output.
  4. Download the compressed copy. Your original stays unchanged.

Important limitation

A target size is a maximum goal, not a guarantee of an exact byte count. Very detailed images, PNG transparency and extremely small targets can make an exact target impossible without unacceptable visual quality. The tool therefore avoids extreme degradation.
