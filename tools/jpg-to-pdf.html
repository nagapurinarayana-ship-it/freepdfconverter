    Page-size choice

    A4 and Letter centre each image on a standard page. Fit makes the PDF page follow the image aspect ratio.

    Make one PDF from photos or scanned pages

    Select one or more JPG or PNG files, put them in the right order and choose the page style. Oversized images are resized in the browser to reduce memory use while preserving their aspect ratio.

    A4 is a good default for documents shared internationally. US Letter is useful for common North American office workflows. Fit works well when you want the PDF page to closely match the source image proportions.

    Quality and privacy notes

    • JPG is best for photographs; PNG is often better for screenshots, diagrams and text-heavy images.
    • The tool never needs to upload your selected images to perform the conversion.
    • Very large camera images may be resized to protect browser memory.
    • Keep the original images until you have checked the generated PDF.

    Read the JPG and PNG to PDF guide for page-size, image-order and quality recommendations.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I mix JPG and PNG files?

    Yes. Both formats can be selected together and will appear as pages in your chosen order.

    Can I change image order?

    Yes. Use the arrow buttons beside the preview thumbnails.

    Are photos uploaded?

    No. Image decoding, resizing and PDF creation happen in your browser.
