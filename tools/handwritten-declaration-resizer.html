Important

Handwritten declaration rules differ by application. Treat the receiving form's current dimensions, file-size, background and format instructions as authoritative.

Trim first

Large margins around a scanned declaration can waste the available image box. A tight crop helps the writing remain readable at the required dimensions.

Background cleanup

Near-white removal can clean paper texture, but faint handwriting may also be affected. Always inspect the prepared preview before submitting.

Prepare handwritten declarations without uploading the source

Use this tool when a form asks for a handwritten declaration as an image with strict dimensions or a maximum upload size. Start from a clear scan or photograph, then match the exact rules shown by the application.

Why trimming and manual framing help

Phone photos and scans often contain unused paper around the writing. Removing those margins before resizing gives the handwriting more useful image area, and the framing editor prevents an off-centre line from being silently cut when an exact output box is required.

Choose the output format carefully

JPG is compact and broadly supported for ordinary form uploads. PNG can preserve transparency and sharp edges. WebP may be smaller, but use it only when the receiving form accepts it.

Keep a visual quality check

Compression and background cleanup should never remove important strokes. Compare the prepared preview with the original before submitting the final file.
